I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way someone shopping for a solar mini split would. The product pages, FAQ and reviews are deep, and your name came up in every search I ran. The leaks are small and specific.

Checked October 16, 2026

What I found

01

“Call or Text” only calls.

Every page's header says “Call or Text (725) 699-1200”, but the link behind it is a plain call link (tel:7256991200). I found no text-message link on the pages I checked, so the link starts a phone call, not a text.

02

Your best-selling product page is slower than your homepage.

In a simulated slow-connection load test, the MS12 page's main image appeared at 4.0 seconds, against 3.0 seconds for the homepage. The test also measured 620 milliseconds during which the MS12 page was too busy to respond to a tap. The page is about 1.8 MB.

03

Your street address is on every page but not in your business markup.

The footer shows 611 N Main St, Ste. 150, Las Vegas, NV 89101. The homepage markup describes an organization with name, links and a sales-and-support contact (phone and email) but no address or hours, so search tools only get the address from the page text.

04

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

On the homepage, two spots are hard to read against their background. On the MS12 page there are seven.

What's working

A fast, well-organized site: in a simulated slow-connection load test the homepage's main content appeared at 3.0 seconds, and my accessibility check scored it 97. Your FAQ has structured data, the MS12 page has product and review markup, and you publish a detailed llms.txt. In all five of my searches you were in the answer, first for “solar mini split air conditioner Las Vegas”.

Unmeasured

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt names ten AI crawlers (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot), but the only addresses it blocks for them are two old redesign mockup pages. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    You publish a detailed llms.txt

    /llms.txt exists and tells AI assistants to cite the most specific page (product, FAQ, reviews, install) rather than the file itself.

  • Found
    FAQ and product markup

    The FAQ and product pages carry FAQ markup. The MS12 page also has product, offer and review markup (a 4.9 rating from 95 reviews).

  • Gap
    Business markup has no address or hours

    The homepage markup is typed as an organization with name, links and a sales-and-support contact point. Your address (611 N Main St, Ste. 150, Las Vegas, NV 89101) is in the footer text only.

  • Gap
    “Call or Text” has no text link

    The header link is tel:7256991200. I found no sms: link.

  • Note
    Old mockup paths in robots.txt

    robots.txt blocks /home-redesign-1/ and /bento-home-redesign-1/. Both return “Page not found” now.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 16, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.
